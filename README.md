# 💫 About Me:
About Me<br><br>I’m Milan. Most of what I build comes from ideas I get in my free time. I like creating things that are practical, fun, or just interesting to experiment with. A lot of my work ends up being tools, bots, or small games that I keep improving over time.<br><br>Personal Projects<br><br>Personal projects are what I spend most of my time on. Some of the things I’ve worked on include:<br><br>A Discord bot for manual video moderation<br><br>Several Minecraft mod experiments<br><br>A hand-tracking mouse controller<br><br>A TikTok Live game that reacts to chat<br><br>An art-submission bot with automated handling<br><br>School projects that I turned into something more than required<br><br>Small AI and computer-vision experiments<br><br>I like starting with a basic idea and turning it into something that actually works, even if it means rewriting things a few times.
