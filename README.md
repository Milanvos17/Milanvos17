# 👋 Hey, I'm Milan

I'm 18, I live in the Netherlands 🇳🇱, and I study Software Development at Teraa in Helmond. I also do an internship at Beginstation, where I've been for a few months. I was already there two years ago, so I guess they like me. Or they forgot to kick me out.

Most of what I build starts as a random idea at an unreasonable hour. Then I rewrite it three times, swear at it a bit, and eventually it works. I mostly make tools, bots and small games that I keep improving way past the point where anyone asked me to.

## 🚧 Currently working on: MilanOS

A personal project that turns my whole house into one big automation system. Lights, TVs, the front door, music, phone, firmware, a wall panel, a speaker: all of it in one repo, all of it talking to each other. It is combined with Jarvis (below), because why would I stop at "smart" when I can go full Tony Stark.

It's a mix of Python, Java, Kotlin, JavaScript, Arduino and ESP32 firmware, and a lot of "why the hell isn't this TV app working".

## 🛠️ Stuff I've built

- **🤖 Jarvis**: a voice assistant for my PC, inspired by Iron Man. It has 100+ tools: it opens apps, controls files and the terminal, searches the web, and can look at the screen and click things on its own. Speech recognition runs locally, and it uses Claude through an API as its brain. It has an animated arc-reactor HUD too, because obviously.
- **🖱️ Hand-Tracking Mouse**: control your mouse with your hand in front of a webcam. Computer vision in Python. Yes, it feels like magic. Yes, it also gets my arm tired.
- **🔁 TwinSYNC**: links my laptop and my PC together, so when I update Linux on my laptop it updates on my PC as well. Lazy? Yes. Efficient? Also yes.
- **⌚ Smartwatch tinkering**: writing my own programs for an old programmable smart watch in C++. Brought it back from the dead.
- **💳 NFC lock**: a school project with an NFC scanner where only one specific card can unlock it. Everyone else gets denied. Sorry.
- **🧠 AI that plays games**: I train AIs in Python to play games. Mostly they're terrible at first, then suddenly they aren't.

Most of my repos are private, so I'm not linking them. Ask me about them and I'll happily talk your ear off.
